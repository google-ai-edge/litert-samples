                How this page works

                Each part is read when the page loads. Trending: the org's models whose trendingScore is above zero, in the order the Hugging Face API returns for sort=trendingScore. Recently created: the org's models by the creation date Hugging Face reports (createdAt), newest first.

                Each of the two lists shows its first 12 models and folds up to 24 more behind a line that says how many. Repos that hold only a README are left out. No one adds a model to these two lists by hand: they follow the sorts on Hugging Face.

                Task is the repo's pipeline_tag, and the source model the base_model of its card. Likes and downloads last month are Hugging Face's counts. A model linked from the picks carries the mark Pick; a model that a sample runs carries the mark Sample.

                Picks: the list on the organization card, shown as written there. It is the list under the card's first heading that has the word "picks", or "pick" when no heading has "picks", up to the next heading of the same level or above. The page shows up to 12 picks, each cut at 1,000 characters, and up to 12 demos.

                Bold, code and links are kept, and HTML tags are removed. A link that leads outside huggingface.co, github.com/google-ai-edge, google-ai-edge.github.io and ai.google.dev is shown as its text.

                Samples: the rows of the sample tables in samples/litert/README.md and samples/litert_lm/README.md whose Model column links a model in either list, folded rows included, or in the picks. Demos: the Spaces of the org's Demos collection, in its order.
