# LiteRT-LM Samples

Usage examples and inference workflows for LiteRT-LM.

## Samples

| Sample | Task | API | Platform | Model |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [`math_to_latex/`](math_to_latex/) | A formula in a photo to LaTeX: drag a box around it, and the phone writes its LaTeX and typesets it, offline | LiteRT-LM | Android (Kotlin) | [litert-community/MiniCPM-OCR-1.0](https://huggingface.co/litert-community/MiniCPM-OCR-1.0), downloaded on first use |
| [`picture_questions/macos/`](picture_questions/macos/) | Questions about a photo or screenshot of a timesheet, an invoice or a price list that take several steps of reading and arithmetic, answered offline | LiteRT-LM (C API from Swift) | macOS (Swift) | [litert-community/Muse-Glimmer-30B-LiteRT](https://huggingface.co/litert-community/Muse-Glimmer-30B-LiteRT) or [litert-community/Qwen2.5-VL-72B-Instruct-LiteRT](https://huggingface.co/litert-community/Qwen2.5-VL-72B-Instruct-LiteRT), downloaded with `hf download` |
| [`reachy-voice-robot/`](reachy-voice-robot/) | A voice robot on a Raspberry Pi: it sees, hears, reasons and speaks | LiteRT-LM and LiteRT | Raspberry Pi (Python) | [litert-community/gemma-4-E2B-it-litert-lm](https://huggingface.co/litert-community/gemma-4-E2B-it-litert-lm) (imported with `litert-lm import`); [litert-community/moonshine-tiny](https://huggingface.co/litert-community/moonshine-tiny) and [litert-community/Inflect-Nano-v2](https://huggingface.co/litert-community/Inflect-Nano-v2), downloaded on first use; a YOLO26 detector exported into `assets/yolo/` |
| [`receipt_to_json/`](receipt_to_json/) | A receipt photo to JSON on the phone, offline: store, date (as YYYY-MM-DD), tax and total, or the keys you type | LiteRT-LM | Android (Kotlin) | [litert-community/Xiaomi-OCR-0](https://huggingface.co/litert-community/Xiaomi-OCR-0), downloaded on first use |
| [`voice_assistant/`](voice_assistant/) | A phone that hears a request, acts on it with its own tools (alarm, timer, calendar) and answers aloud, offline | LiteRT-LM and LiteRT | Android (Kotlin) | [litert-community/gemma-4-E2B-it-litert-lm](https://huggingface.co/litert-community/gemma-4-E2B-it-litert-lm), [litert-community/Zipformer-medium-CR-CTC-LiteRT](https://huggingface.co/litert-community/Zipformer-medium-CR-CTC-LiteRT) and [litert-community/kitten-tts-nano-0.8](https://huggingface.co/litert-community/kitten-tts-nano-0.8) with the G2P files of [litert-community/Matcha-TTS](https://huggingface.co/litert-community/Matcha-TTS), downloaded on first use |
| [`offline_planner/`](offline_planner/) | One typed sentence becomes calendar and message actions on the app's own data; the app shows each one with a check and runs them when you tap Run, offline | LiteRT-LM | Android (Kotlin) | [litert-community/LFM3-1.7B-LiteRT](https://huggingface.co/litert-community/LFM3-1.7B-LiteRT) (`LFM3-1.7B_mixed_int4_ekv2048.litertlm`), copied to the phone with adb |
| [`where_to_put/`](where_to_put/) | A phone that marks where to put something in a photo of a table, a desk or a shelf, offline: one point for a request such as "Put the mug between the lamp and the books." | LiteRT-LM | Android (Kotlin) | [litert-community/LFM3-VL-2B-LiteRT](https://huggingface.co/litert-community/LFM3-VL-2B-LiteRT) (`LFM3-VL-2B_int4_ctx4096.litertlm`), pushed with adb |
| [`click_or_code/`](click_or_code/) | The next step on a screenshot of a desktop, offline: type what you want done, and the app answers with a point to click or a shell command, and runs nothing | LiteRT-LM (Kotlin on Android, C API from Swift on macOS) | Android (Kotlin), macOS (Swift) | [litert-community/Mage-VL-Cowork](https://huggingface.co/litert-community/Mage-VL-Cowork), downloaded in the app |
| [`voice_to_text/`](voice_to_text/) | Speak in English, French, German, Spanish, Portuguese or Japanese, and the phone writes down what you said, with punctuation, offline; the recording never leaves the phone | LiteRT-LM | Android (Kotlin) | [litert-community/granite-speech-5.0-2b](https://huggingface.co/litert-community/granite-speech-5.0-2b), downloaded on first use |
